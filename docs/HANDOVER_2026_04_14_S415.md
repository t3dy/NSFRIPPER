### 1. Bankswitch Emulation Fix (scripts/nsf_to_reaper.py)
Two bugs found and fixed in the py65 NSF emulator:
- Bug 1: Non-page-aligned load addresses (Ninja Gaiden $FC00, Zelda $8D60, etc.)
  shift all bank boundaries. ROM data is NOT padded. Fix: virtual padded array

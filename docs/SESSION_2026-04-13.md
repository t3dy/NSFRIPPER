## Driver Family System (architecture rule 18)

Five families based on CC11/CC12 density per note:

| Family | CC11/note | CC12/note | Example Games | Count (pre-survey) |
|--------|-----------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| 1: Hardware Envelope | 0.0-2.8 | < 0.5 | Mega Man, DuckTales, 1942 | ~31 |
| 2: Standard Envelope | 2.8-5.6 | < 0.5 | CV1, Contra, Battletoads | ~14 |
| 3: Duty Animators | 3.7-4.9 | 0.7-1.0 | SMB1, CV3 US, Kirby | ~4 |
| 4: Dense Automators | 5.1-14.9 | < 0.5 | FF, Blaster Master, Batman | ~13 |
| 5: Full Animation | > 7.0 | > 1.0 | SMB3 | ~5 |

(Counts are from collection_state before the driver survey re-run completes.
50 games were "unknown" — the survey will classify them.)

## Fidelity Hierarchy (unchanged)

1. Mesen Trace (APU register dumps from gameplay)
2. SysEx in MIDI (lossless register encoding)
3. NSF emulation (6502 CPU, per-frame CC11/CC12)
4. CC11/CC12 in MIDI (volume + duty envelope)
5. ADSR approximation (keyboard mode only)

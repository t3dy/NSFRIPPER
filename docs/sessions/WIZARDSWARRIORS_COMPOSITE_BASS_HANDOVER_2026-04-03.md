## Current strongest conclusions

### 1. The short disputed event is present in the title MIDI

The saved title MIDI contains same-pitch retriggers in the disputed phrase.

Around the phrase:

- pulse1 track:
  - frame `928`: `note_off 57`, `note_on 57`
  - frame `960`: `note_off 57`, `note_on 57`
  - frame `976`: `note_off 57`, `note_on 55`
- triangle track:
  - frame `928`: `note_off 45`, `note_on 57`
  - frame `960`: `note_off 57`, `note_on 57`
  - frame `976`: `note_off 57`, `note_on 55`

Important implication:

- the issue is **not** "the note is absent from MIDI"
- the issue is that the articulation/body is still wrong even though the
  retrigger exists

### 2. There is no hidden `$4015` mute/gate command in the disputed phrase

Frame write capture for `920-980` shows writes to:

- `$4000/$4002/$4003` for pulse1
- `$4004/$4006/$4007` for pulse2
- `$4008/$400A/$400B` for triangle

There are **no `$4015` writes** in this phrase window.

Important implication:

- the missing pluck/mute effect is not explained by a direct channel enable
  toggle

### 3. Pulse1 is definitely part of the heard pluck

At frames `928`, `960`, and `976`:

- pulse1 rewrites `$4003`
- pulse1 is in envelope mode: `$4000 = 0x45`

Decoded:

- duty `1`
- loop/halt `0`
- constant volume `0`
- envelope period `5`

Important implication:

- pulse1 is not a steady square in this phrase
- it is an envelope-driven attack voice and is likely carrying much of the
  "harpsichord" / pluck onset

### 4. Triangle is still being over-flattened in playback

In the phrase:

- triangle repeatedly uses `$4008 = 0x81`
- current playback still treats triangle largely as:
  - on if `$4008 & 0x7F > 0`
  - off otherwise

That is too coarse.

Important implication:

- the current synth still gives triangle too much steady body
- this likely masks the short plucked/muted effect the user is hearing in the
  game audio

### 5. The reference audio supports a composite interpretation

From the release IR:

- frame `928` = `fresh_full_body`
- frame `960` = `fresh_attack_damped_body`
- frame `976` = `fresh_full_body`

At `960` specifically:

- `audio_high_norm = 1.0`
- `audio_low_norm = 0.4814`
- `composite_attack = true`

Important implication:

- frame `960` behaves like a bright fresh attack with reduced bass body
- that fits "pulse pluck over damped low support" better than
  "another full triangle bass note"

## Best current interpretation

The disputed bass/pluck phrase is likely a **composite instrument**:

- `pulse1` supplies the percussive pluck / harpsichord-like onset
- `triangle` supplies low support/body
- at frame `960`, the audio has the attack but not a full renewed low body

So the present playback failure is probably:

1. pulse1 pluck still not strong or hardware-shaped enough
2. triangle body still too steady and too dominant

That combination produces exactly what the user described:

- synth blob instead of a plucked/harpsichord note
- bass that rings too long and masks the short event
